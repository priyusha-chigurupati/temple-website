Sacred History & Architecture

Founded during the height of the regional dynasties, AnkammaThalli Temple stands as a testament to the intricate Dravidian architectural style and the enduring spirit of local traditions.

The temple is dedicated to Goddess Ankamma, the fierce yet benevolent protector of the village. For generations, devotees have sought solace within these walls, finding a rare blend of historical majesty and intimate spiritual peace.
