Our History

Founded on the principles of selfless devotion and communal harmony, the AnkammaThalli Temple has stood for nearly two centuries as a beacon of spiritual guidance in the heart of our community.

What began as a small stone shrine under the shade of an ancient Banyan tree has evolved into the majestic architectural marvel you see today. Every carving tells a story, and every stone carries the prayers of generations past.

In the early 20th century, the temple underwent a massive transformation under the guidance of master craftsmen from the southern regions, introducing the intricate Gopuram and the sacred inner sanctum that remains the spiritual heartbeat of the temple.

"The temple is not just a structure of stone, but a living testament to the faith that binds us across generations."

The Legacy Lives On

Today, we welcome thousands of devotees every week, maintaining the same traditions that our ancestors established centuries ago.
